Employee Workforce & Salary Analysis

Dashboard documentation · Excel

Project overview

This Excel dashboard presents workforce headcount, gender distribution, departmental composition, demographic categories, job titles, organisational divisions and annual salary information. It brings several HR-related measures together in a visual report for exploration.

Tool: Microsoft Excel.

Dashboard features

  • Employee headcount and gender distribution.
  • Department-level employee comparisons.
  • Distribution across four displayed demographic categories.
  • Job-title treemap and organisational-division donut chart.
  • Employee-level annual salary listing.
  • Filters for year, department and country.

Questions this dashboard can help explore

  • How is the workforce distributed across departments?
  • How does the displayed gender distribution vary by department?
  • Which job titles occupy the largest visible areas in the treemap?
  • How are employees distributed across the displayed organisational divisions?
  • Which employee records show the highest listed annual salaries?

Preliminary observations

  • Female employees represent 51.3% of the displayed workforce and male employees 48.7%.
  • The department chart shows different headcounts across the departments displayed.
  • The dashboard includes employee-level salary values and several workforce segmentation visuals.

These are observations of the dashboard, not conclusions about pay equity, hiring patterns or historical changes.

Limitations

The underlying Excel workbook and source dataset are not available for verification. The exact calculations, data-cleaning steps, year-range coverage and salary aggregation definition have not been independently validated. The title shows 1990–2022, but the visible filter labels are truncated.

This case study documents the visible dashboard. No unverified data preparation steps or business outcomes are claimed.
