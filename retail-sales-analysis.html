Retail Sales Performance Analysis

1. Business Question

Which product categories and regions contribute most to sales and profit, how has performance changed over time, and which sub-categories may need a profitability review?

2. Dataset & Tools

  • Source file: Sample-Superstore.csv provided for this analysis.
  • Coverage: 9,994 order-line records, with order and shipping dates spanning 2014–2017.
  • Fields used: Order ID, dates, region, category, sub-category, sales, quantity, discount, and profit.
  • Tools: Excel-oriented dashboard design and summary analysis; the supplied CSV was used to calculate the figures.

3. Key Performance Indicators

Total sales, total profit, profit margin (total profit divided by total sales), unique orders, and unique customers were calculated from the dataset.

5. Regional Performance

The West was the highest-sales region at $725,458, followed by the East at $678,781. Regional sales and profit are included in the workbook's Region Summary tab.

Sales increased from $484,247 in 2014 to $733,215 in 2017. This is an observed trend in the dataset, not proof of the cause.

7. Sub-category to Investigate

Tables had the lowest total profit at $-17,725.48. Bookcases and Supplies also recorded negative total profit. These are candidates for deeper analysis of discount levels, unit economics, and costs; the dataset alone does not establish the cause.

8. Recommendations

  1. Review discounting and margin by product and order for Furniture, especially Tables and Bookcases.
  2. Compare category-level profit margin alongside sales before prioritizing growth campaigns.
  3. Examine regional and yearly trends monthly to identify when performance shifts occur.
  4. Validate any operational changes against current, business-owned data before making decisions.

9. Deliverable
